## Failure and edge cases

Accountability can pass syntactically while mapping a valid ID to the wrong feature. Review semantics, not just counts. A green historical CI result must not satisfy a current-build gate. Unsupported platform or missing school-owner evidence stays NOT ASSESSED. Financial forecasts/pricing remain hypotheses; no commercial launch work is inferred.
