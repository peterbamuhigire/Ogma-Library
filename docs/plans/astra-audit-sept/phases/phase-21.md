## Exit gates

- Independent score >=95/100 on the frozen denominator, every category >=90%, zero P0/security/privacy/data-loss/critical accessibility blockers.
- All mandatory Windows/macOS, reference-hardware, signing, recovery, user and school-controller gates accepted; no NOT ASSESSED item silently converted to pass.
- Observed user-task success and AI quality meet the declared sample contracts, with raw evidence and negative cases accessible to reviewers.
- Pilot observation window closes without unresolved stop-trigger events; candidate digest matches the accepted release.
- Every finding is closed with evidence, explicitly retained as a nonblocking residual risk, or governed by an approved scope change.
- Owner and reviewer sign-offs identify what was accepted, limitations, support owner and next review date.

## Recovery and continuous improvement

On failure, return the specific phase to triage; do not rescore around it. Keep candidate unpublished or roll back the pilot with verified data recovery. Schedule reviews7/30/90 days after release as proposed checkpoints, and immediately after privacy/security incidents, model changes or major platform updates. Recheck official model releases/runtime catalogue during each Kaizen cycle and retain Peter's policy unless he authorizes a verified change.

The standard to teach is simple: a feature is complete when its user can use it, its failure can be recovered and its claim can be traced to evidence.
